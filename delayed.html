HOW IT WORKS

THE IPAD RECORDS. THE IPHONE ONLY SENDS SHORT COMMANDS (“BUTTON PRESSED”), NO VIDEO.

DIRECT PAIRING:
1. ON THE IPAD PRESS PAIR IPHONE.
2. SCAN THE IPAD QR CODE WITH THE IPHONE CAMERA AND OPEN THE LINK.
3. ON THE IPAD PRESS STEP 2 AND POINT THE IPAD CAMERA AT THE QR CODE ON THE IPHONE.
4. START THE RECORDING ON THE IPAD AND PRESS A PERSON'S BUTTON ON THE IPHONE.

IF NOTHING WORKS, TAP THE SCREEN OF THE PERSON ON THE IPAD INSTEAD.
